Privacy notice.

BYTEWORLD's MVP does not require accounts, wallet connections, or personal information. Contract addresses and network selections submitted for analysis are sent to this application's server, which requests on-chain data from the configured RPC endpoint and checks Sourcify for verified metadata. Those services may receive request metadata under their own policies.

Reports are generated in the browser and are not stored by this application. This starter site does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. If you operate a deployment, update this notice to describe your hosting, logging, and provider retention practices.
